Welcome to the Corridor. Before you move through, a quick orientation.

Start with Ms. Ivy — she runs the SLR Method on your concept and maps the gaps. Then Dr. Rao issues the dual use GO or NO-GO. After that, the eight specialist offices are yours to work through. Each one maps a different surface of your concept's operational security.
